## How to Add the Next Case Study

When I finish a new project, here's exactly what I do to turn it into a portfolio case:

### Steps (takes ~20 min)

1. **Open my Claude Project** ("Backend Security Portfolio — AI Fluency"). It already knows my voice card, proof statement, stack, and tone. No setup needed.

2. **Run the interview prompt** (saved in the Project knowledge):
   ```
   I want to turn a new project into a case study. Interview me one question at a time:
   what problem it started from, what I did and decided, what I tried that didn't work,
   what came of it, what I'd do differently. Don't write yet. Start.
   ```

3. **Answer honestly** — even if the result was small or I made mistakes. Those details are what make it sound like a real person.

4. **Shape into three beats** — tell Claude: "Turn my answers into the three beats: problem, what I did, what came of it. Keep my words. Include the one thing I'd do differently."

5. **Edit hard** — read the draft out loud. Cut any word I wouldn't say to another engineer. Swap anything that sounds like generic AI copy.

6. **Add to portfolio** — paste into the Work section. Update the homepage if this is a stronger project than the current first one.
